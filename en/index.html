Your business needs

Move the core forward. Preserve business value.

Insurance core modernization involves business logic, data and the way teams operate. Start by identifying what needs to change and which connections need to remain.

01

Accumulated business value

Business rules, data and operational knowledge need to keep serving the business throughout modernization.

02

Connected applications

A core change can require updates to front ends and integrated systems. Its impact needs to be understood.

03

Inconsistent user experience

Menus, forms and data tables differ between business functions, forcing users to relearn how to work.

04

Controlled transitions

Each change needs a defined scope, accountable owners and acceptance criteria; transactions with an unclear outcome must be visible for reconciliation.

NOVA is designed for this approach: preserve the value that matters, introduce new capabilities and verify each step.

Four solution families

Four solution families. Orbit–Vista at the centre.

Orbit connects and coordinates existing business capabilities, opening modern ways to use them with limited deep changes to the core. Vista provides one consistent workspace. Together they form the central axis and first delivery focus; Nexus and Add-ons belong to the same model.

How to get started

Assess one insurance business function before expanding.

Agree on what needs to be demonstrated, how to measure it and the conditions for delivery. Assessment results provide a basis for choosing the next investment scope.

  1. 01

    Establish the current state

    Choose the insurance business function, the core system and connected applications, then identify the priority problem.

  2. 02

    Define the assessment

    Agree on compatibility requirements, test data, measurement methods and acceptance criteria.

  3. 03

    Verify the results

    Compare business results, performance and operational effort with the current state for the same scope.

  4. 04

    Recommend the next step

    Identify what can proceed or needs further verification, with requirements and an indicative investment scope.
