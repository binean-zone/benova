Echelon · Vista · Aice

One process. People, services and AI complete it together.

EVA is Binean's controllable operating model for business processes. Echelon holds the flow of work, Vista brings each Task to the responsible person, and Aice performs or assists with the work suited to AI.

The problem

Real processes always cross people and systems

A case does not simply pass through APIs. It waits for a person to review it, asks AI to read documents, calls a business service, then returns for a decision. When each participant uses a separate mechanism, state fragments and accountability becomes hard to follow.

↗

Handoffs break easily

Email, spreadsheets, queues and scripts each hold a different part of the same case. Nobody sees the full journey.

◎

AI becomes a black box

A model output without its source data, reviewer and processing state is difficult to trust in real operations.

⌁

Automation gets locked in

Changing one step from a person to AI or a service often means rebuilding the flow, integrations and controls around it.

The EVA model

Three layers, one closed loop of work

EVA makes a clear separation between orchestration, interaction and execution. Each layer owns its part while using Tasks and Outcomes to work together.

E

Echelon

Orchestration engine

Holds Flow and Process state, emits Tasks, receives Outcomes and decides the next step from one source of authority.

  • Long-running, versioned processes
  • One Task for a person, service or AI
  • Event-based recovery and inspection
V

Vista

The human workbench

Presents the Task, data and actions a person needs. Vista captures user intent without making business decisions on its own.

  • Tasks, forms and context in one place
  • Clear processing state
  • An interface shaped by the Flow
A

Aice

AI capability

Takes a complete Task suited to AI, or drafts an answer inside a Human Task for the responsible person to review, edit and confirm.

  • Extraction, classification and summary
  • Drafts that match the output schema
  • Human control where it matters

Task → Outcome

One contract from end to end

Changing the executor does not require a new Flow: a person can handle it today, Aice can assist tomorrow, and a service can take over once the rules are clear.

  1. 01

    Echelon emits a Task

    The Flow defines the work and its input data.

  2. 02

    Vista or Aice executes

    A person, AI or service receives exactly the work assigned.

  3. 03

    The Outcome returns

    Result and status come back through the same contract.

  4. 04

    Echelon navigates

    Basal chooses the next step and preserves one source of truth.

Value

Automation grows without giving up control

EVA is designed for processes that need both machine speed and human judgement.

◫

One inspectable journey

The Task, executor, result and transition live in one event trail instead of being scattered across tools.

✓

People keep important decisions

Aice can draft and recommend; the responsible person still confirms outcomes with material consequences.

⇄

Handover when ready

A Task can move from a person to AI and then a service as the data, confidence and rules become clear enough.

⌂

Designed for private environments

The architecture aims to run on infrastructure the organisation chooses when data cannot leave its control boundary.

Applications

Start with one process that has cases, rules and a reviewer

EVA fits work with clear input, several stages and a need to preserve evidence for the final decision.

01

Case intake and validation

AI extracts and checks data while people focus on exceptions and the final decision.

02

Multi-stage approvals

Services prepare data, Vista brings the Task to the right role, and Echelon keeps the conditions and history.

03

Investigation and incident response

Machines collect signals, AI assembles context, and a person approves action before the system continues.

A strong pilot starts with one Flow, one case type and one integration, with measurable success criteria.
