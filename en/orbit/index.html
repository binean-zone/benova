Product status

The runtime is running; NOVA integration is in progress.

Orbit has a working foundation. Its end-to-end value still needs development and verification.

INITIAL FOUNDATION

A basis for further development

  • A flow engine that runs business scenarios
  • Web services and an adapter for existing connections
  • Backend session management with Aster
  • Authentication and token design

DIRECTION

Expand value for the business

  • Customize connection gateways for each system
  • Connect Vista screens with business flows
  • Verify compatibility and performance before expanding
  • Alongside Vista and Aster in NOVA

Orbit is part of the NOVA package under development and is not a ready-to-deploy replacement for the core. Scope and timing are discussed per requirement.
